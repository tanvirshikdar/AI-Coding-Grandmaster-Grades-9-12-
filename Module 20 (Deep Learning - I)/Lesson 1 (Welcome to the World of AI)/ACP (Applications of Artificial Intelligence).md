## Application 1: Virtual Private Assistants (Alexa)

### Overview
Virtual assistants like **Amazon Alexa** are AI-powered software agents that can perform tasks or services for an individual based on commands or questions. They use a combination of Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Machine Learning to understand and respond to human speech.

### How it Works
1.  **Speech Recognition:** Alexa converts the sound waves of your voice into text.
2.  **Natural Language Understanding (NLU):** The AI analyzes the text to understand the intent (e.g., "What is the weather?" means the user wants a forecast).
3.  **Machine Learning:** The more you speak to Alexa, the better it becomes at recognizing your specific accent and vocabulary.

### Impact on Daily Life
Alexa has revolutionized smart homes by allowing users to control lights, play music, set reminders, and order groceries using only their voice. It makes technology more accessible for people with visual or motor impairments.

## Application 2: Autonomous Vehicles (Self-Driving Cars)

### Overview
Self-driving cars, such as those developed by **Tesla** or **Waymo**, use Artificial Intelligence to navigate through traffic and reach destinations without human intervention. These vehicles are equipped with a suite of sensors and powerful AI algorithms.

### How it Works
1.  **Computer Vision:** Cameras around the car capture images of the surroundings. AI models (Neural Networks) identify objects like pedestrians, traffic lights, and other cars.
2.  **Sensor Fusion:** Data from cameras is combined with LiDAR and Radar to create a 3D map of the environment.
3.  **Path Planning:** The AI calculates the safest and most efficient route, making split-second decisions on when to brake, accelerate, or steer.

### Impact on Daily Life
Self-driving technology aims to reduce traffic accidents caused by human error (such as fatigue or distraction). In the future, it could lead to more efficient traffic flow and provide mobility to those who are unable to drive themselves.
